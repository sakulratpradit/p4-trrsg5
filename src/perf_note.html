How to read it:

1. Calendar years, 1 Jan - 31 Dec; 2025 is a part year from 15 Jul. Stocks only - SGOV and cash are left out, as in the lifetime tile. Dividends are not included.
2. Return % (Modified Dietz) - the professional way to measure a year when new money keeps arriving: gain ÷ (start value + each month's money in, weighted by how much of the year it was invested). Money that arrived in December cannot be expected to earn a full year.
3. "Same money" index columns - every dollar we put in is assumed to go into SPY or QQQ (dividends included) in the same month, then measured exactly like ours. If the index number is higher, an index fund would have beaten us that period. Today we trail the S&P 500 in 2026 so far and since the start.
4. Since start: 5.5% vs 9.4% a year. 5.5% is total gain ÷ total money in. Most of the money went in during 2026 and has been invested only a short time, so 9.4% a year (money-weighted, allowing for when each dollar arrived) is the fairer since-start rate.
5. Automatic. A 2027 row appears on 1 January; each year closes on the 31 Dec value in the daily history, and the next year starts from it.
6. Known small gaps: the since-start gain differs from the lifetime tile by $180 (June cost difference, ANET and TER); the since-28-Jul row uses the trade-by-trade ledger and differs from the monthly ledger by about $1,666 (Aug/Sep dating).

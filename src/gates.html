Rate gates - why 35x and why 5.25%

Filed 13 Nov 2026 at Salee's request. Explains the two rate rules approved on 11 Nov 2026: the valuation gate (35x forward earnings while the 10-year is at or above 5.00%) and the rate tripwire (10-year close above 5.25%). Figures are at the 12 Nov close.

Every step reads the closing 10-year yield, never an intraday print. All three apply to new money only and never force a sale.

2. Why 35x forward earnings

Earnings yield. Turn a P/E upside down and you get what the company earns for every $100 you pay.

Earns per $100 invested
US 10-year Treasury at 5.00%$5.00, guaranteed
Our board's average stock (18.6x forward)about $5.40
A stock at 35x forwardabout $2.86

At 35x you start $2.14 a year behind a risk-free bond. Earnings must grow 1.75 times just to match it:

Earnings growth a yearYears to reach the bond's 5%
25%about 2.5
20%about 3
15%about 4

When the bond pays 4%, waiting is cheap. At 5% or more, every year of waiting costs real money.

High-P/E stocks fall hardest when yields rise. Most of a 35x stock's value is profit many years away, and money far in the future is worth less when rates rise. A 35x stock is roughly twice as rate-sensitive as our 18.6x average.

Why 35 and not 30 or 40 - a judgment line. 40x would miss ISRG and GEV, the only names with unspent room, so the gate would do nothing. 30x would block names priced for ordinary growth. 35x catches the top ~17% of holdings by value and leaves the core alone.

Escape hatch. A stock above 35x can still be bought at a written price line, because the line already prices it sensibly (PLTR at 90.6x is exempt at its $150 line).

4. The tripwire - a 10-year close above 5.25%

1. All buying stops except the standing price lines (TSM $380, AVGO $310, APP $320, PLTR $150 - APP and PLTR still wait for the February money).
2. The February $100,000 parks in SGOV on arrival and waits until the 10-year closes back below 5.25%.
3. Nothing is sold.

Why 5.25%. Our stocks earn about 5.4% on average. At 5.25% the bond pays nearly the same with no risk, so buying more stocks earns almost no extra reward - unless a stock falls to its price line and becomes cheap enough to beat the bond.

5. Review

Keep 35x and 5.25% as set; review both at the 10 Dec review (the Fed decides 8-9 Dec US, about 02:00 on 10 Dec Bangkok). What would change the setting: if the board's average earnings yield fell below 5.00% (average forward P/E above 20x), 5.25% would be too loose and the tripwire should move down to about 5.00%.

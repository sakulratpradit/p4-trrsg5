Expats leaving Bangkok - what it means for our rentals (Pillar 2)

Filed 28 Nov 2026 at Salee's request. Source: Thai Facebook page post (about 27 Nov 2026), graded Noted - one family's story, not data. Filed here because it bears on the condo and house tenants in Pillar 2; it changes nothing on the US board.

1. What the post says (English)

"Westerners can't take it! A family packing up to leave Bangkok for good. They spent ages touring neighbourhoods across Bangkok looking for somewhere quiet for the family, but nearly every area has construction going on or new towers coming. They say it isn't about cost of living, since they can afford a comfortable life. They were beaten by air pollution, noise and traffic that money can't fix."

2. Why it matters to us

The expat tenants who pay the most are exactly the ones who care about these problems and can afford to leave. A single post proves nothing, but it names the three risks that decide whether a good tenant renews: construction next door, air quality, and traffic.

3. What to do

At every renewal or move-out, ask why the tenant is leaving, and record any mention of noise, air, construction or traffic. Count them; if it becomes a pattern, it is data, not a post.
If the pattern shows, price to keep sitting tenants (a small rent hold beats a vacancy month) rather than chasing new ones.
Air purifiers in the family units are a small, cheap retention tool - worth offering at renewal.
Before signing a long lease, check for planned towers or road works next to the building.

Bottom line: no action today. Track move-out reasons from now on.

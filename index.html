Risk sensitivity how much our holdings move when the S&P 500 or the 10-year yield moves · regression on daily closes · refreshed monthly

Plain reading: beta 1.5 = when the S&P 500 moves 1%, this stock has typically moved 1.5% the same way. Bad-day beta = the same, measured only on days the S&P 500 fell. Rate = % move per +0.10 point rise in the 10-year yield, with the market held constant. Fit = how much of the stock's daily moves the S&P 500 explains (low fit = the stock follows its own news). These describe the past, they do not predict prices.
